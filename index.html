About Me

I’m a Software Engineer and Brigham Young University graduate who enjoys turning ideas and real-world challenges into useful digital products. I’m interested in the whole process: understanding what people need, shaping a clear and intuitive experience, and building it with reliable, maintainable code.

My experience includes JavaScript, React, Node.js, C#, .NET, and Python. I’ve worked on projects ranging from an administration application to a website for the Mashonaland Central Basketball Association, as well as this portfolio. Each has helped me strengthen my approach to problem-solving, thoughtful design, and building for the people who will use the finished product.

I’m also enthusiastic about cybersecurity and the role it plays in creating software people can trust. I want to keep growing as an engineer, take on meaningful challenges, and build secure, scalable applications that make a practical difference.
